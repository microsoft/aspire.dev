## Developer-icon permission

The [original, line, and plain developer icons](/community/brand/logos/#developer-icons) are official derivatives for identifying Aspire in developer icon libraries, including Simple Icons, Devicon, and Nerd Fonts. They can also identify Aspire in documentation and README badges, editors, terminals, and other developer tools.

Permission is granted to use, reproduce, and redistribute these variants for those purposes. You may proportionally resize them, render them in monochrome or recolor them, and optimize their SVGs or normalize their paths, provided the recognizable Aspire shape and proportions are preserved.

Do not imply Microsoft or Aspire endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation. This narrow exception does not permit arbitrary redesigns or changes to other brand assets.

See the <BrandExternalLink href="https://github.com/microsoft/aspire-brand/tree/main/logo/developer-tools#developer-icon-usage-permission">original developer-icon usage permission</BrandExternalLink>.
